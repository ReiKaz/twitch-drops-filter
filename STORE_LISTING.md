# Opera Addons listing text

**Name:** Twitch Drops Filter

**Short summary (≤ 132 chars):**
Filter Twitch Open Drop Campaigns by reward type (badge, emote, item) and requirement (subscribe, watch).

**Full description:**
Twitch Drops Filter adds a small "Filter drops" panel to twitch.tv/drops/campaigns so you can find the drops you want without opening every campaign by hand.

• Filter by reward: Badge, Emote, In-Game Item
• Filter by requirement: Subscribe, Watch, Other
• Scan all campaigns automatically (with Stop and Pause)
• Hide games with no matching reward, and optionally hide the Closed campaigns section
• Choose scan order: newest first or ending soonest first
• Click a result to jump to that game on the page
• Your settings are remembered

Private by design: no tracking, no analytics, no external requests. Everything runs locally in your browser and only on the Twitch Drops campaigns page.
