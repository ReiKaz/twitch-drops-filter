# Privacy Policy – Twitch Drops Filter

Twitch Drops Filter does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data.

- **What it reads:** the content of the Twitch Drops campaigns page you are viewing, only to filter it in your browser.
- **What it stores:** your filter settings (chosen reward types, requirements, hide options, scan order), saved locally in your browser with the `storage` permission.
- **Network:** the extension makes no requests of its own and sends no data anywhere.
- **Permissions:** `storage` (save your settings) and access to `https://www.twitch.tv/*` (the only site it runs on).

The extension is unofficial and is not affiliated with Twitch.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
